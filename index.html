LesomTech — automotive sunshade manufacturing, Dongguan, China

Dongguan Saisi Auto Accessories Technology Co., Ltd. (brand LesomTech) manufactures automotive sunshades in Dongguan, China. How a panel is built

Performance comes from the stack: face fabric, reflective or heat-absorbing layer, backing and edge construction. The stack is agreed before sampling. Panels are cut on a flatbed cutter, bound and stitched on computerized sewing programs, bonded where the build calls for it, then accordion-folded to fit the agreed pouch. Frame brackets and related metal parts are punch-pressed in-house. Packing follows a fit check on the target vehicle.

Ordering

  • Production starts at 100 pieces per style; sample quantities, charges and requested tests are confirmed separately.
  • A mixed order across several designs is not one style for the 100-piece minimum.
  • Fitment must be confirmed against a target vehicle and an approved sample.
